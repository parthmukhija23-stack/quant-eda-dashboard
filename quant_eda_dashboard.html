All pair correlations sit in a very weak band between 0.03 and 0.26. Most notably, pairing CONSUFAST and MEDICURE at r = 0.03 offers exceptional empirical potential for portfolio variance reduction without sacrificing expected return.

1. Risk ≠ Reward

The riskiest stock by absolute volatility wasn't the top performer. While PETROMAX had high volatility (34.2%), it yielded a negative return (-0.9%), proving volatility alone doesn't guarantee premium return.

2. Fat-Tailed Returns

Every stock exhibited leptokurtic distributions with heavier tails than standard Gaussian models predict. CONSUFAST recorded the highest excess kurtosis (6.3x), signaling frequent tail risk.

3. Diversification Works

Low cross-asset correlations (0.03 to 0.26) across the 5 sectors provide measurable empirical proof for Markowitz modern portfolio theory, effectively dampening portfolio risk.

4. Volume Predicts Shocks

Cross-referencing abnormal daily price moves revealed that every extreme outlier return day consistently coincided with 2x to 4x surges in average trading volume.

5. Drawdowns Hurt Everyone

Even the star performer TECHNOVA—which rallied nearly 9x—suffered a severe peak-to-trough maximum drawdown of -28.6% during market corrections.

6. Volatility Clustering

Financial volatility is non-constant over time (heteroskedasticity). Large price variations tend to cluster around earnings and news cycles rather than occurring randomly.
